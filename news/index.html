2026

  • May 2026
    Adjustable-equilibrium parallel elastic actuator (AE-PEA)Our paper, published in IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters, introduces an adjustable-equilibrium parallel elastic actuator that combines a direct-drive motor and a 3D-printed torsional spring with a small auxiliary motor for continuous equilibrium adjustment. The design integrates high torque control bandwidth, passive energy storage, shock mitigation, and equilibrium tuning in a single actuator.

2025

  • Sep 2025
    Learning Finite-Horizon Optimal Control with Unknown Control-Affine DynamicsOur paper, published in Systems & Control Letters, introduces a method for learning finite-horizon optimal control without requiring a model of the system dynamics. The approach approximates time- and state-dependent optimal control policies through model-free relearning of simple linear policies, with proven convergence and optimality under persistent excitation.

    Three papers accepted at IEEE ICRA 2023Three of our papers have been accepted for presentation at the IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation in London. The three designs are the subject of pending patent applications.
