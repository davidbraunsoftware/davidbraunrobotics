Privacy policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026 · Last updated: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit davidbraunrobotics.com (“Website”) and how it is used.

Who we are

This Website is owned and operated by David Braun. You can reach us through the contact form.

No cookies, no tracking

This Website does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising tools, and it does not create visitor profiles. The fonts are served from this Website itself.

Information you provide: the contact form

When you send a message through the contact form, we receive your name, your email address, and your message. The form is delivered by the service Web3Forms, which forwards the message to us by email. Web3Forms processes this information only to deliver the message; please see the Web3Forms privacy policy.

We use this information only to answer your message. We do not add you to any mailing list.

Information collected automatically: the hosting provider

This Website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you open a page, GitHub’s servers technically receive your IP address, the page requested, the time, and your browser type. GitHub uses this information to deliver the pages and to keep the service secure (GitHub Privacy Statement); we do not use it to identify you.

Videos and links to other websites

Some pages show preview pictures of videos hosted on YouTube; these pictures are stored on this Website. YouTube is contacted only when you click a video, which then opens on YouTube. Links to other websites (for example journals, Google Scholar, ORCID, or LinkedIn) are contacted only when you click them; those websites have their own privacy policies.

Sharing your information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. It is shared only with the services named above, as far as needed to run this Website and deliver your message, or if required by law.

How long we keep your information

Messages you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer them and for our records of that correspondence.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. To do so, please use the contact form.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
